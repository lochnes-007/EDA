
## EDA Process (ThinkStats Book)

- **Ask a question:** Define the population, outcome, and explanatory variables.
- **Collect / load data:** Open the provided datasets and notebook (`nsfg.ipynb`, `nsfg.py`, `thinkstats.py`).
- **Summarize distributions:** Compute PMF/CDF and show histograms for key variables.
- **Summary statistics:** Report mean, median, variance, percentiles, and standard error.
- **Visualize:** Use histograms, CDFs, boxplots, and scatterplots to reveal patterns.
- **Examine relationships:** Use scatterplots, correlation, and contingency tables to explore associations.
- **Stratify:** Compare subgroups to reveal conditional differences.
- **Detect issues:** Look for outliers, missing values, and data-entry problems; clean as needed.
- **Resample & uncertainty:** Use bootstrap/resampling to estimate variability and confidence intervals.
- **Interpret & report:** Relate results back to the original question and note limitations.

Follow these steps iteratively: visualization and summary statistics often suggest new questions.
